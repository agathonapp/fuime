The arithmetic

Four hundred dollars, after the multiplier.

The $400 test is not run against your profit. It is run against your profit times 92.35%, the Schedule SE adjustment for the employer-equivalent half of self-employment tax. The profit that actually trips it is $433.14.

fuime used to compare raw net profit to $400, which told every business between $400 and $433 that it owed when it may not have. The number on this page is the corrected one, and the specificity is the whole point: $400 is the rule, $433.14 is where the rule bites.

Income is every ledger line that reads as a customer paying you. Expenses are the lines going the other way, fuime's own fee included. Net profit is the difference, kept running all year rather than assembled in April. Money you put into the business yourself, and transfers between your own accounts, are excluded on a best-effort basis, because overstating what somebody owes is the more harmful mistake to make.

What it tracks

Income, expenses, net profit, all year.

Four numbers, kept current by the ledger itself rather than typed in by you at the end of the year.

Income, as it actually arrives

Every sale posts its own ledger line, so the year's income is what was collected and not what was billed. An invoice nobody paid stays outstanding and never counts toward the threshold.

Expenses, with the receipt attached

What you spend on the business sits beside what you earned, with the receipt paired to the transaction. You can upload one or email it in, and it lands on the right line.

Net profit, updated as you go

Income minus expenses, recomputed whenever the ledger moves. The page tells you how much more profit would reach $433.14, which is a more useful sentence in June than in April.

A packet somebody else can read

A CSV of income, expenses, net profit, net earnings and the threshold, stamped as an estimate on its own first line. A guardian on the account can download it and hand it on.

Where it stops

Six things this does not do.

Other platforms say they handle tax. fuime does not handle tax. It estimates one number about your own income, and here is everything that sits outside it.

It does not file anything

No return, no extension, no quarterly payment. fuime shows you a number and that is where its job ends. Whether you file, and what you file, is between you and whoever prepares it.

It does not touch checkout

The estimate is read off the ledger after the money has already moved. Nothing about it changes what a client is charged, what the payment page shows, or what the receipt says.

It does not calculate or collect sales tax

No sales tax, no VAT, no GST, at checkout or anywhere else. fuime holds tax registrations in zero jurisdictions and files no return in any of them. If something you sell is taxable where you sell it, that is not handled here.

It only knows what it can see

A job paid in cash, a W-2 shift, a second platform: none of it reaches this number. The estimate covers your fuime ledger and nothing outside it, which is usually not the whole of your year.

It is an estimate, not a determination

Income is classified by heuristic, not by a chart of accounts. State income tax, information reporting and quarterly estimated payments are not modelled. No accountant has signed off on the arithmetic, and the app says so where it shows it.

It is not tax advice

fuime is not a tax preparer and not an advisor. The page hedges on purpose: it says what your ledger shows and what the threshold is, and stops before telling you what you owe.

Questions

The ones worth asking in June.

Why $433.14 and not $400?

Because the $400 test applies to net earnings from self-employment, and net earnings are net profit times 92.35% — Schedule SE deducts the employer-equivalent half of the tax before the test is run. $433.14 times 92.35% is $400.05, which is the first dollar of profit that clears it.

Does fuime file anything for me?

No. Not a return, not a schedule, not a quarterly payment. fuime produces a packet you can download and hand to whoever does your return, and that is the entire extent of it.

What about money I earn outside fuime?

It is not in the number. fuime can only total the transactions in your own fuime ledger, so cash work, a W-2 job and any other platform sit outside the estimate. Your actual position is the sum of all of them, which is one of the reasons this is not a determination.

Does fuime charge my clients sales tax?

No. There is no tax line at checkout and no tax collected on any sale. fuime is registered in zero tax jurisdictions and remits nothing to any of them. A platform that says it handles tax for you is describing something fuime does not do.

What happens to the number when I refund a client?

The refund posts as a reversal on the ledger and fuime's fee is rebated in proportion, so income falls and so does net profit. A partial refund books the increment rather than the running total, which keeps the year-to-date figure honest instead of double-counting.

Who else can see this?

A guardian on the account has read access to the ledger, the same numbers, and the packet download. Where a business is run by somebody under 18 that is the point of it: the person who has to answer for the tax position can read it without asking.

Is any of this tax advice?

No, and it is written to avoid sounding like it. fuime is not a tax advisor. Take the packet to somebody who is, and treat every number on the page as an input to their work rather than an answer.
