The yearly version

An offer can renew once a year, for a client who would rather settle at once.

Each renewal

The charge posts itself. So does the record.

You do not re-send anything. A renewal is a sale like any other, with the same ledger line and the same visible fee.

It charges on schedule

On the same date each month or year, at the price the offer was published at.

It posts to the books

A ledger line as it settles, with fuime's fee on its own negative line.

It is a normal transaction

Attach a receipt, pair one that arrived by email, or argue in the comments.

Nothing is owed on money that never came

The fee comes out of what collects. There is no monthly charge.
