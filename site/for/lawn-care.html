How yard work gets paid now

Cash, a neighbour, and nothing written down.

You finish the back edge, sweep the path, knock, and he says he will get you next week. Nothing anywhere records that the lawn was cut at all.

Cash, folded, or not at all

It arrives in a hand or it does not arrive. Either way there is no record that the job happened, so the only ledger is the one you are keeping in your head between houses.

"I'll get you next week"

You have no invoice to point at, so following up feels like begging. The next Saturday you are back in the same garden doing the same work on credit you never agreed to extend.

A neighbour, not a client

He has known you for years, so sending a bill feels like changing the relationship. You do not send one, and the money stays a favour being owed in both directions.

No scope, so no argument you win

The hedge came out shorter than he pictured, or a sprinkler head went under the blade. With nothing agreed in writing, the person who is more embarrassed pays for it.

The part nobody says

The line you may have crossed

$400 is closer than a summer of Saturdays.

The IRS expects a return at $400 of net self-employment income. Schedule SE multiplies net earnings by 92.35% first, so the real trigger is $433.14 of net profit.

It counts the year as it goes

Income in, expenses out, running net, checked against the threshold. You find out in August where you stand, rather than reconstructing eleven gardens from memory in April.

$433.14, not $400

The 92.35% multiplier is the reason the two numbers differ, and most people who quote the $400 have never applied it. fuime applies it, and shows you the number it used.

It is an estimator, and only that

It does not touch checkout, does not calculate sales tax, and does not file anything with anyone. Nothing in it is tax advice, and it will not tell you what to do next.

A packet somebody can hand over

Where the person doing the work is under 18, the guardian on the account can download a tax packet for the year and give it to whoever prepares the return.

Questions

What people ask on the way in.

Is mowing something fuime can even sell?

Yes. Mowing, edging, hedge work, leaf clearing, gutters and hauling are services, and fuime sells services and digital goods. What it cannot sell is anything physical you hand over: plants, mulch, food or crafts.

My clients pay cash. Why change?

Because cash leaves you nothing. No proof the job was done, no receipt for a client who wants one, no record of a year's work when somebody asks where the money came from, and no document to point at when a payment is late.

What does it cost me?

5% of what you collect, with a 50¢ floor. It is a floor rather than an additive fee: on a job you price at $400 the fee is $20.00, not $20.50. No monthly fee, nothing until a sale, and unlimited businesses, and API keys are included rather than unlocked. Card processing of about 2.9% + 30¢ is paid by fuime out of that 5% and never added to your side. Anything outside the standard rate is a conversation with sales — the full breakdown.

Can I bill the same garden every month?

Yes. An offer can bill monthly or yearly, and the renewals post to the ledger by themselves. One thing to say out loud first: a client cannot cancel it themselves yet — they email support@fuime.com and a person ends it. How recurring works.

Who is my client actually buying from?

Ninth Street Labs, LLC, doing business as Fuime, is the seller of record on the purchase, and Stripe processes the card. Their statement will say Fuime, so it is worth telling them that before the first payment rather than after — how that works.

When does the money reach me?

Payout runs are generated weekly and a person approves every one before anything moves. The policy is a 7-day hold, a 10% rolling reserve over 90 days, a $2,500 cap per run and a $10 floor, and any operator skipped gets a stated reason. There is no automated rail and no promised date.

Do I have to be 18?

No. From 13 up, a parent or legal guardian accepts a versioned agreement and becomes the account holder and legal payee, and you run the round. Nobody's identity is verified: the signature IP, user-agent, timestamp and document version are recorded, and that is all. Under 13 is refused at signup.

Where this stops

Four things fuime will not do for you.

Worth knowing before you move a round of regulars onto it, because none of these come up during sign-up.

It will not set your prices

There is no suggested rate, no recommended range, and nowhere in the product to store one. What a garden is worth is between you and the person who owns it.

It will not chase anybody

You still send the link and you still knock. What changes is that you are pointing at a priced offer instead of asking a favour, and an unpaid one costs you nothing.

Nobody's identity is checked

There is no identity verification of anyone in fuime, and age is a self-attested checkbox. Vetting is a person reading your application before the business can sell. No document is collected, checked or held.

Nothing physical, nothing abroad

Services and digital goods only, in dollars, in the United States, in English. Selling the plants you put in, or billing anyone outside the country, is outside what fuime does.
