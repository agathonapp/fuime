Questions

What a tutor asks first.

Can I sell tutoring on fuime?

Yes. A session is a service, and a worksheet pack or a recorded walkthrough is a digital good. Childcare and babysitting are excluded, and so are physical goods, food and crafts.

What does an hour of tutoring cost me?

5% of what you collect, with a 50¢ floor. It is a floor, not an additive fee: on a block you price at $400 the fee is $20.00, not $20.50.

Card processing of about 2.9% + 30¢ is paid by fuime out of that 5%, never added to your side. Anything outside the standard rate is a conversation with sales. The full breakdown.

Who is the student's family buying from?

Ninth Street Labs, LLC, doing business as Fuime, is the seller of record, and Stripe processes the card. What you hold is a claim on fuime for what you earned, not a balance.

Why that matters.

When does the money for a term reach me?

Payout runs are generated weekly and a person approves every one before anything moves. The policy is a 7-day hold, a 10% rolling reserve over 90 days, a $2,500 cap per run and a $10 floor.

Every operator skipped in a run gets a stated reason. There is no automated rail and no promised date.

Do I have to be 18 to tutor for money?

No. From 13 up, a parent or legal guardian accepts a versioned agreement and becomes the account holder and legal payee; you run the tutoring. Under 13 is refused at signup.

Nobody's identity is verified. The signature IP, user-agent, timestamp and document version are recorded, and that is the whole of it.

What happens at tax time?

fuime totals income, expenses and net for the year against the IRS $400 self-employment threshold. Schedule SE multiplies net earnings by 92.35% first, so the real trigger is $433.14 of net profit.

It is an estimator: it does not touch checkout, calculate sales tax, or file anything. What it counts.

Can I raise my rate?

You set every price and change it whenever you like. fuime has no suggested rate, no recommended range and nowhere to store one. It will never tell you what your hour is worth.

Where this stops

Four things fuime will not do.

Not a tax service

It estimates your income tax position. It files nothing, and none of it is advice.
