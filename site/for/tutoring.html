Set it once, on the offer

The same offer you would sell one time can bill monthly or yearly instead. You choose the interval and the amount when you publish it.

Renewals post themselves

Each month's charge lands as its own ledger line with its own fee line, its own receipt and its own comment thread. You do not have to remember to invoice anybody.

A client cannot cancel it themselves

Not yet. To stop a subscription they email support@fuime.com and a person ends it. Tell a client that before you set one up, because finding it out later is worse than knowing now.

A refund gives the fee back

Refunding a month posts a reversal and rebates fuime's fee in proportion. A partial refund books the increment, not the running total, so the books do not drift.

Questions

What a tutor asks before signing up.

Can I actually sell tutoring on fuime?

Yes. A session is a service, and fuime sells services and digital goods — a worksheet pack or a recorded walkthrough counts as well. Childcare and babysitting do not, and neither do physical goods, food or crafts.

What does it cost me?

5% of what you collect, with a 50¢ floor. It is a floor and not an additive fee: on a session you price at $400 the fee is $20.00, not $20.50. No monthly fee, nothing until a sale, and unlimited businesses, and API keys are included rather than unlocked. Card processing of about 2.9% + 30¢ is paid by fuime out of that 5% and is never added on top, because fuime is the legal seller and Stripe bills fuime. Anything outside the standard rate is a conversation with sales rather than a tier — the full breakdown is here.

Who is my client actually buying from?

Ninth Street Labs, LLC, doing business as Fuime, is the seller of record on the purchase. Stripe processes the card. What you have in the app is a claim on fuime for what you earned, not a balance — why that matters.

When do I get the money?

Payout runs are generated weekly and a person approves every one before anything moves. The policy is a 7-day hold, a 10% rolling reserve over 90 days, a $2,500 cap per run and a $10 floor, and every operator skipped in a run gets a stated reason. There is no automated rail and no promised date, so this page will not give you one.

Do I have to be 18?

No, and that is the unusual part. From 13 up, a parent or legal guardian accepts a versioned agreement and becomes the account holder and legal payee; you operate the business. Nobody's identity is verified — the signature IP, user-agent, timestamp and document version are recorded, and that is the whole of it. Under 13 is refused at signup.

What happens at tax time?

fuime totals income, expenses and net for the year and tells you where you stand against the IRS $400 self-employment threshold. Because Schedule SE multiplies net earnings by 92.35%, the real trigger is $433.14 of net profit. It is an estimator: it does not touch checkout, does not calculate sales tax, and does not file anything.

Can I raise my rate?

You set every price and you can change it whenever you like. fuime has no suggested rate, no recommended range and nowhere to store one, so it will never tell you what an hour of your teaching is worth. That is deliberate.

Where this stops

Four things fuime will not do for you.

Worth reading before you move a client onto it, because none of these turn up in the sign-up flow.

It will not chase anybody

You still send the link and you still follow up. What changes is that you are pointing at an invoice instead of asking a favour, and an unpaid one costs you nothing.

Nobody's identity is checked

There is no identity verification of anyone in fuime, and age is a self-attested checkbox. Vetting is a person reading your application before the business can sell. No document is collected, checked or held.

It is not a tax service

The tracker estimates your income tax position and flags the threshold. It does not calculate sales tax, file a return, or give advice, and nothing here is tax advice.

USD, and the United States

Sales are in dollars and the product is English-language and US-only. A client abroad, a second currency, or a rate quoted in anything else is not something fuime can do.
