Questions

The ones a programme lead asks.

Do I need a guardian signature per student?

No. A school stands in loco parentis, so a cohort needs no per-student guardian.

How young can a student be?

13. The floor is hard-clamped there, and under-13 is refused at signup.

What can students sell?

Services and digital goods, in US dollars, in the United States. Not physical goods, food, crafts, childcare or babysitting.

What does it cost the programme?

5% of what a venture collects, 50¢ floor. Not an addition: a $400 sale pays $20.00. It posts as its own negative line, so a class can read the margin. Outside that rate, talk to us.

How long before a student can sell?

A person reads every new business before it can take a payment. We will not promise a review turnaround, so tell us your term dates.

What about tax?

fuime totals income, expenses and net against $433.14 of net profit, the real IRS trigger. It estimates; it files nothing.

After the course ends?

The venture keeps existing. Its books and customers belong to it; a student carrying on needs a parent or guardian.

fuime is a financial technology company, not a bank. fuime does not hold deposits and does not offer FDIC-insured products. Payments are processed by Stripe. Ninth Street Labs, LLC, doing business as Fuime, is the seller of record on purchases. Guardians are the legal payee on operator payouts and must approve the destination before the first payout; young founders run the venture day to day.
