Questions

What a shooter asks before switching.

Can I take money before the shoot and the rest after?

Yes, two ways. Publish the booking amount as its own named offer and the balance as another, or ask the buyer for a free-chosen amount and let them enter the figure you agreed. Both post to the ledger as ordinary sales.

Can I sell prints, albums or a USB?

No. fuime sells services and digital goods only. The shoot, the edit and a delivered gallery are fine. A printed album, a framed piece or anything that ships in a box is not, and neither are food or crafts.

Who is my client actually buying from?

Ninth Street Labs, LLC, doing business as Fuime, is the seller of record on the purchase, and Stripe processes the card. The charge on their statement says Fuime, which is worth mentioning to a client before the shoot rather than after — how that works.

What does it cost me?

5% of what you collect, with a 50¢ floor. It is a floor rather than an additive fee: on a job you price at $400 the fee is $20.00, not $20.50. No monthly fee, nothing until a sale, and unlimited businesses, and API keys are included rather than unlocked. Card processing of about 2.9% + 30¢ is paid by fuime out of that 5%, never added to your side, because fuime is the legal seller and Stripe bills fuime. Anything outside the standard rate is a conversation with sales — the full breakdown.

What if I have to refund a client?

A refund posts a reversal on the ledger and rebates fuime's fee in proportion, so you are not paying a platform fee on money you gave back. A partial refund books the increment, not the running total.

When does the money reach me?

Payout runs are generated weekly and a person approves every one before anything moves. The policy is a 7-day hold, a 10% rolling reserve over 90 days, a $2,500 cap per run and a $10 floor, and any operator skipped in a run gets a stated reason. There is no automated rail and no promised date, so this page will not invent one.

Do I have to be 18?

No. From 13 up, a parent or legal guardian accepts a versioned agreement and becomes the account holder and legal payee, and you run the business. Nobody's identity is verified: the signature IP, user-agent, timestamp and document version are recorded, and that is all that is kept. Under 13 is refused at signup.

Where this stops

Four things fuime will not do for you.

Read these before you move a booked client across, because none of them turn up during sign-up.

Nothing physical, nothing abroad

Services and digital goods only, in dollars, in the United States, in English. A print order, a second currency or a client in another country is outside what fuime can sell.

No contract, no model release

fuime gives you a priced offer, a receipt and a comment thread. It does not draft your terms, hold a signed release or decide who owns the files. That paperwork stays yours.

Nobody's identity is checked

There is no identity verification of anyone in fuime, and age is a self-attested checkbox. Vetting is a person reading your application before the business can sell. No document is collected, checked or held.

No sales tax, and no filing

fuime is registered in zero jurisdictions and files nothing. The tax tracker estimates your own income tax position against the IRS threshold and stops there. None of it is tax advice.
