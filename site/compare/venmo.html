It is not a record.

This is the comparison most small sellers are actually making, and it is not close on speed. fuime does not beat a transfer app at moving money between two phones. It beats it at the year afterwards.

Go to the table

What each one gives you, and where fuime is the worse pick.

What Venmo is better at

Start with what it wins.

Any comparison that opens with the other option's faults is selling. Venmo is the default way people hand each other money for a reason, and none of these three is a small thing.

It is already on the phone

Your client has it. You have it. Nobody signs anything, nobody is reviewed, and nobody has to explain to a customer what the name on their statement means.

There is nothing to set up

No business to name, no agreement for anyone to accept, no review before you can take the first payment. fuime asks for all three, and that is a real cost on day one.

It is the right tool for small and personal

Splitting a check, paying back a friend, taking $20 from somebody standing in front of you. fuime is not better at any of that and is not trying to be.

Nobody is in the middle

An app moves money between two people. fuime puts a company in the middle as the legal seller, which is what makes a receipt possible and also what makes it cost something.

What you give up

The payment is fine. The aftermath is not.

Nothing here is about the transfer itself, which works. It is about the four things that do not exist once it has gone through.

It is somebody's personal account

It's her account, her name, her transaction history. You ask her for your own money. On your own account it is your name, and your business income is filed in the same place as your groceries.

There is no invoice to point at

A request in an app is a nudge. An invoice with a number, a date and a line item is a document, and chasing one stops feeling like begging the moment it exists.

There is nothing to hand a client

Business clients ask for receipts, and a screenshot of a phone is not one. On fuime every sale produces a real receipt, and you can upload or email in the ones you are given.

There is nothing at tax time

fuime totals income, expenses and net for the year and flags the IRS $400 self-employment threshold as you approach it. A year of app transfers is a reconstruction job in April.

Where fuime stops

When you should keep using the app.

fuime is worse than a transfer app on six things, and pretending otherwise would make the rest of this page worth less.

It costs money

5% of what you collect, minimum 50¢, with no monthly fee and nothing until a sale. On a $5 sale the minimum binds and that is 10%, which is worth knowing before you list one.

You cannot start this afternoon

A human at fuime reads every new business before it can sell, and there is no promised turnaround because there is no queue we would be honest quoting.

Under 18, an adult has to sign

A parent or legal guardian accepts the Guardian Agreement and becomes the account holder and legal payee. Nothing is paid out until they have. That is a person you have to ask.

Money does not move immediately

There is a 7-day hold, a 10% rolling reserve over 90 days, a $2,500 cap per payout run and a $10 floor, and a human approves every run. A transfer app has none of that.

Only some kinds of work

Services and digital goods, in USD, in the US. Not physical goods, food or crafts. Childcare, babysitting and coaching children are excluded deliberately.

Not for splitting a dinner bill

fuime has no reason to exist for paying back a friend. If the payment is personal, it belongs in the app you already have and this page is not trying to move it.
