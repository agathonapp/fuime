What you are not charged for

Six things that never turn into a bill.

There is no second page to the pricing. If it isn't your plan's platform fee, Pro's $15 a month, or Stripe's 2.9% + 30¢ on a card payment, it isn't a fee.

No setup fee

Signing up costs nothing. Naming the venture costs nothing. The agreement your guardian accepts costs nothing.

No fee on an invoice that never gets paid

The platform fee comes out of money that arrives. If a client ghosts you, you lose the work. You do not also pay us for it.

No fee to send an invoice

Send one this year or forty this week. There is no per-invoice charge and no cap on how many you write.

No minimum

Nothing to clear before the account works. A $25 job goes through the same way a $2,500 one does.

No contract

Nothing to sign for a term and nothing to cancel out of. Stop sending invoices on Standard and the price goes back to $0.

No charge for the guardian's account

Their login, the read-only view of every invoice, and the year-end summary are all part of it. Pro's $15 is the only subscription that ever reaches them.

How the whole thing works

Questions

The ones worth asking before you sign up.

What happens if a client never pays?

You owe nothing. The platform fee is charged on money that arrives, so an unpaid invoice costs you the chasing and nothing else. fuime keeps it in the books as outstanding, not as income, so your year-end total stays honest.

Why 7% on Standard and not less?

Because there is no floor underneath it. No setup fee, no per-invoice charge, and no monthly bill on Standard at all. When you are small, the percentage is paying for all of it. If you are collecting enough that 7% hurts, Pro trades $15 a month for a 4% fee and comes out cheaper above about $500 collected in a month.

Who pays fuime's fees?

The parent or legal guardian who owns the account, on the card attached to it, because they are the account holder and the person on the hook for it. Never the young founder. Nothing is billed at all during the private beta, because payments run in Stripe test mode and no real money moves.

What does Stripe charge on top?

2.9% plus 30 cents on a card payment, taken by Stripe out of the payment. That is Stripe's price for processing a card, every business that takes one pays it, and it is charged on top of whichever fuime plan you are on — the 7%, the 4%, or the 0%. It is shown as its own line on the invoice so the number you read is the number that is left.

Does fuime ever hold the money?

Today no real money moves at all: fuime is in private beta and payments run in Stripe test mode, into a Stripe account fuime controls, with nothing settling anywhere. fuime is a financial technology company and not a bank. At live launch the design is a Stripe account owned by your parent or legal guardian, so the payment goes from your client to your family and the platform fee is collected as a fee on that payment rather than as a balance we sit on. That is the plan we are building, not the state of things right now.

What happens when I turn 18?

Nothing resets. Your invoices, clients and books stay attached to the account, and the plan and its price stay the same. What we have not built yet is moving the account itself out of your guardian's name and into yours. We will say so here when we have.
