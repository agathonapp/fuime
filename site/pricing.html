How the whole thing works

Questions

The ones worth asking before you sign up.

What happens if a client never pays?

You owe nothing. The fee is charged on money that lands in your account, so an unpaid invoice costs you the chasing and nothing else. fuime keeps it in your books as outstanding, not as income, so your year-end total stays honest.

Why 7% and not less?

Because there is no floor underneath it. No setup fee, no per-invoice charge, and no monthly bill until the business clears $250 in 30 days. When you are small, the percentage is paying for all of it. A cheaper rate with a fixed fee attached would cost you more in every month you barely worked, which for most people starting out is most months.

Who pays the $15 a month?

The guardian, on the card attached to the account, because they are the legal owner of it. It is charged only in a month where more than $250 came in during the previous 30 days. Below that the account is $0 and stays fully working.

What does Stripe charge on top?

2.9% plus 30 cents on a card payment, taken by Stripe before the money reaches your account. That is Stripe's price for processing a card and every business that takes one pays it. It is shown as its own line on the invoice so the number you read is the number that lands.

Does fuime ever hold the money?

No. fuime is not a bank. The payment goes from your client into the Stripe account your guardian owns, and our 7% is collected as a fee on that payment. We never sit in the middle holding a balance, and there is no fuime wallet for money to get stuck in.

What happens when I turn 18?

Nothing resets. Your invoices, clients and books stay attached to the account, and the price stays the same: 7%, and $0 a month under $250. What we have not built yet is moving the Stripe account itself out of your guardian's name and into yours. We will say so here when we have.
