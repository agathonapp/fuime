How the whole thing works

Questions

The ones worth asking before you sign up.

What happens if a client never pays?

You owe nothing. The platform fee is charged on money that arrives, so an unpaid invoice costs you the chasing and nothing else. fuime keeps it in the books as outstanding, not as income, so your year-end total stays honest.

Why 5%, and not less?

Because there is nothing underneath it. No setup fee, no per-invoice charge, no monthly bill, and no upgrade to buy — unlimited businesses and API keys are included. Card processing comes out of that 5% rather than being charged to you on top, which is most of where it goes. The 50¢ is there because a card payment costs us about 30¢ whatever its size, so without it a $3 sale would cost us money to process.

Who pays fuime's fees?

The parent or legal guardian who is the legal payee, because they are the person on the hook for it. Never the young founder.

What does card processing cost on top?

Nothing extra. Because fuime is the merchant of record — the legal seller on the receipt — Stripe charges its 2.9% plus 30 cents to us, not to you, and it comes out of our 5%. What you are owed is the sale minus the fuime fee, and that is the number the invoice shows.

Does fuime ever hold the money?

Client payments settle to Fuime's payment processor account because Fuime is the seller. Ninth Street Labs, LLC is the seller of record. What you see in the app is an amount owed to your venture (a payable), not a bank balance. Payouts go to a destination your guardian approves. fuime is a financial technology company and not a bank.

What happens when I turn 18?

Nothing resets. Your invoices, clients and books stay attached to the account, and the plan and its price stay the same. What we have not built yet is moving the account itself out of your guardian's name and into yours. We will say so here when we have.
