Receipts

Two ways in, one place they land.

A receipt that is not attached to anything is a receipt you will not find. Pairing is the step that turns a file into evidence, and it is the step everyone skips when it is manual.

Upload it

Email it in

Ask about it in the thread

A refund, line by line

Money going back posts as its own event.

A refund does not edit the sale. It posts a reversal and rebates fuime's fee in proportion, and a second partial refund books the increment rather than a recalculated running total.

The fee is 5% of what you collect, minimum 50¢, so $20.00 on a $400 sale. Give a quarter of the sale back and a quarter of the fee comes back with it. Stripe's 2.9% + 30¢ is paid by fuime out of that 5% and is never added to your side of it.

The whole price, with the arithmetic

Why it matters

April is a bad time to remember March.

Books reconstructed after the fact are a guess with a spreadsheet around it. Books written as the money moved are the thing you hand someone when they ask a question you did not expect.

A client asks for a receipt

You have the transaction, the date, the amount and the offer they bought. It takes a minute, rather than an evening of scrolling back through a chat looking for a number.

Somebody asks what you made

Income, expenses and net for the year are already totalled, because every line was written at the moment it happened and nothing had to be reconciled afterwards.

The $400 question

fuime estimates net income against the IRS $400 self-employment threshold with the 92.35% Schedule SE multiplier, so the real trigger is $433.14 of net profit. You hear about it when you cross it.

Someone else needs to read it

Where the seller is 13 to 17, the parent or legal guardian who holds the account has read access to the ledger and can download a tax packet. That is the one place age shows up in the books.

What fuime does and does not do about tax

Where the books stop

Six things fuime will not do with your numbers.

A ledger that overstates what it is doing for you is worse than no ledger, because you stop reading it. Here is the edge of it, stated before you find it yourself.

It files nothing

fuime is not a tax preparer and submits nothing to anyone. It shows the numbers and flags the milestone. What gets filed, and by whom, is a question for someone qualified to answer it.

It does not calculate sales tax

No sales tax, and no arithmetic pretending to be it. fuime holds tax registrations in zero jurisdictions and is not going to imply otherwise on a page about bookkeeping.

It is not advice

Nothing in the books and nothing on this page is tax or accounting advice. It is arithmetic performed on transactions that actually happened, which is a different thing.

It does not know about money it never saw

A job paid in cash on someone's driveway is not in these books unless you put it there. The ledger is honest about what went through it and silent about everything else.

US dollars, in the US

One currency and one country. There is no second ledger in another currency, no conversion, and no version of this that works for a client paying from abroad.

It shows what you are owed

The figure in the app is what fuime owes your business after fees and refunds, which is a payable. Getting it sent to you is a separate step with a person in it.
