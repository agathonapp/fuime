Getting started

Whether this is for you at all.

fuime is a merchant of record for small businesses selling services and digital work. It also takes sellers aged 13 to 17, which is the part almost nothing else does.

Who can use fuime?

Anyone in the United States selling a service or digital work. Sales are in US dollars and the product is English only. If you are between 13 and 17 you can use it as well, which almost nothing else allows, as long as a parent or legal guardian accepts the Guardian Agreement.

What can I sell, and what can't I?

Services and digital goods. Tutoring, photo and video, web work, design, DJ sets, lawn and yard care, a file somebody downloads. Not physical goods, not food, not crafts, and not childcare, babysitting or coaching children. Those are excluded on purpose rather than pending.

How does a client actually pay me?

You publish a named offer at a set price between $1 and $10,000. It gets its own payment page, and the business gets a public storefront page you can send people to. Checkout is hosted by Stripe. You can also ask the buyer to choose the amount instead of setting one, and renaming an offer later does not break a link you have already sent.

Is there anything to install, or to wait for?

Nothing to install. You sign up, name the business, and a person at fuime reads the application before it can sell. We are not going to tell you how long that takes, because there is no promise sitting behind the number. A seller under 18 also needs a guardian to accept the agreement before anything is paid out.

Can I run more than one business?

Yes, as many as you want, and as many API keys as you want with them. Both are included in the price rather than sold as an upgrade, so a second business costs exactly what the first one did: nothing until it sells something.

Money and fees

One price, and where every cent of it goes.

There is one rate and there is no tier above it. The only numbers on this site are the ones the billing code actually uses.

What does fuime take?

5% of what you collect, with a 50¢ floor. It is a floor and not an addition: on a $400 sale the fee is $20.00, not $20.50. On a small sale the floor is the part that bites, so a $5 sale pays 50¢, which works out at 10%. The fee never comes to more than the sale itself.

Is there a monthly fee, or anything before I sell?

No. No monthly fee, no setup fee, no per-invoice charge and no minimum to clear. Nothing is billed until money actually arrives, and an invoice a client never pays costs you nothing at all.

Does card processing cost extra on top?

No, and this is the part most platforms get to be vague about. fuime is the merchant of record, so Stripe bills Ninth Street Labs, LLC for its roughly 2.9% + 30¢ and that comes out of fuime's own 5%. What you are owed is the sale minus the fuime fee. There is no second deduction underneath it.

What if the standard rate doesn't fit what I sell?

Then it is a conversation rather than a tier. There is one price and nothing to upgrade to, so real volume or an unusual shape of sale means custom pricing worked out with a person. Email support@fuime.com. There is also a founders cohort at 0%, by invitation.

Can I bill someone every month?

Yes. An offer can bill monthly or yearly and renewals post to the ledger by themselves. One thing to know before you sell one: a buyer cannot cancel a fuime subscription themselves. They email support@fuime.com and a person stops it. That is a gap, and it is on the roadmap as one.

Do I owe tax on this?

Probably, and fuime tells you when it starts to matter. It totals income, expenses and net for the year against the IRS $400 self-employment threshold, and because Schedule SE applies a 92.35% multiplier the real trigger is $433.14 of net profit. A tax packet can be downloaded. It is an estimator for income tax: it calculates no sales tax, it files nothing, and it is not advice.

When it goes wrong

The answers people put off asking.

Who your client is buying from, who carries the risk, and what nobody at fuime checks. These are the ones worth reading before you send your first link.

Who is my client actually buying from?

Ninth Street Labs, LLC, doing business as Fuime. fuime is the merchant of record, which means it is the legal seller on the sale and its name is the one on your client's card statement, as Fuime* followed by your business name. What you hold is a claim on fuime for what you have earned, and fuime pays it out.

Does anyone check who I am?

No. There is no identity verification of anyone, anywhere in fuime. Your age is a checkbox you tick yourself. The only gate is a person reading a new business's application before it can sell, and that is a review of an application and nothing more. We would rather write that down than let you assume a system exists that does not.

Can I use fuime if I am under 18?

Yes, from 13, and it is the part almost nothing else allows. A parent or legal guardian accepts a versioned Guardian Agreement and becomes the legal payee; you operate the business day to day. You can set everything up and you can sell while you wait, but nothing is paid out until they have accepted, and a minor can never approve their own payout. Under 13 is refused at signup.

What happens if a client charges back?

The money comes back out, and fuime's fee comes back with it in proportion. The dispute itself is decided by your client's card issuer, not by us. Where the seller is a minor, the guardian is the legal payee and the clawback obligor, so it lands with them. That is the trade for being able to take a card at all, and it is better weighed now than discovered later.

What if a client never pays?

You owe nothing. The fee is charged on money that arrives, so an unpaid invoice costs you the chasing and nothing else. fuime keeps it in the books as outstanding rather than as income, so the year-end total stays honest.

What happens when I turn 18?

Nothing resets. Your clients, your ledger and your books stay where they are and the price stays the same. What has not been built is moving the account itself out of a guardian's name and into yours. It is listed as missing rather than as done, and it will move when the code does.

What runs today, and what doesn't

Where fuime stops

Four things it does not do.

If one of these is what you need, fuime is the wrong tool today and we would rather you find that out here than after your first sale.

It is not a bank

fuime is a financial technology company, not a bank. It holds no deposits and offers no FDIC-insured product. The number you see is what fuime owes you, which is a different thing from money sitting somewhere in your name.

It does not touch sales tax

fuime is registered in zero jurisdictions. It calculates no sales tax, collects none, remits none and files nothing. The tax feature that exists estimates your income tax for the year and stops there.

It is US only, in dollars only

No second country, no localised checkout, no multi-currency pricing and no settlement in anything but the dollar. A client who wants to pay in their own currency cannot.

Nothing ships

Services and digital goods only. There is no shipping address, no inventory and no fulfilment anywhere in the product, so anything that has to arrive in a box cannot be sold through it.
