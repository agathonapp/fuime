How fuime works

Three steps. One of them needs a parent.

01

Sign up and name your business

About a minute. You'll need a parent or guardian's email address, because of step two.

02

Requires a guardian

A parent completes one ID check

Stripe lets a guardian own an account on behalf of someone 13 or older. They verify once and they're the legal owner. You're the operator: you send the invoices, you spend the money, you run the business.

03

Invoice clients and get paid

Send a link. Clients pay by card or bank transfer. The money settles into your account, and fuime keeps your books as it goes: income, expenses, net, and a warning when you cross the $400 the IRS cares about.
