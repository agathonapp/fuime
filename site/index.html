The problem

The work is real. Getting paid for it isn't.

Tutoring, lawn care, photo shoots, design, DJ sets. The client is real and so is the money, but it arrives through a group chat and somebody else's account.

Venmo to a parent

Their account, their name, their history. You ask for your own money.

Cash

No record, no receipt for the client, nothing to show at tax time.

“I'll get you next week”

With no invoice to point at, following up feels like begging.

Taking cards yourself

A merchant account, a business entity, a contract. Under 18 you can't sign one.

What fuime is

fuime sells for you, then pays you what you earned.

Your client buys from fuime and pays by card. fuime pays you the sale, less one fee, and keeps the books while it does. We are the seller of record, so the payments paperwork is ours, not yours.

Company

What we're building Questions rushil@fuime.com

fuime is a financial technology company, not a bank. fuime does not hold deposits and does not offer FDIC-insured products. Payments are processed by Stripe. Ninth Street Labs, LLC, doing business as Fuime, is the seller of record on purchases. Guardians are the legal payee on operator payouts and must approve the destination before the first payout; young founders run the venture day to day.
