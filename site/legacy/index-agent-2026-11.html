Where things stand.

Fuime is pre-launch. This is what's true today, and what will be true when we open.

Who holds the money
A partner bank, Member FDIC. We'll name it here once the agreement is signed. Fuime is a financial technology company, not a bank.
How it's protected
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Sign-in takes two steps, and any card can be frozen from the app.
What exists today
The product is in development. There are no customers or deposits yet, which is why you won't see any numbers about them here.
What's next
A pilot with about 100 businesses, targeted for Q2 2027. Fees will be published before it opens.

FAQ

Anything else: support@fuime.com

What can the AI agent do, and can I use it now?

It's built to set up rules, automations and dashboards from plain-language requests: setting aside taxes, paying suppliers, chasing invoices, sorting spending. It's in development and comes after the account opens. This page shows the direction, not a finished feature.

Is Fuime a bank?

No. Fuime is a financial technology company. Banking services will be provided by a partner bank, Member FDIC, which we'll name once the agreement is signed.

Who can open an account?

Startups, small businesses, sole proprietors and freelancers in the US. Every applicant goes through identity and business verification, which the law requires.

Another app turned me down. Should I still apply?

Yes. Small and early businesses are who we're building this for. We can't promise approval, but we'll look at your business on its own terms.

When can I open an account?

We're planning a pilot with about 100 businesses, targeted for Q2 2027. Join the waitlist and we'll email you when it opens.

What will it cost?

Pricing isn't set yet. We'll publish it before the pilot opens.

Is there a web app?

Not at first. Fuime starts as a mobile app, and a web app follows.
