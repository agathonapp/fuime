# Fuime: context brief for the fuime.com rebuild

## What Fuime is
Fuime is business banking for startups and small businesses, including the sole proprietors and very small businesses that the big startup banking apps often turn away. Customers open a business operating account online, send and receive ACH and wires, use company debit cards, and (coming) accept card payments from their own customers.

Think "modern business bank account built for small operators," not a teen product and not a consumer app. Older Fuime messaging about teen banking is out of date; do not use it.

## Product shape
- Mobile app first. The web app comes after. The website's main job is to explain Fuime, build trust, and get people on the waitlist / download list.
- Core features at launch:
  - Business checking / operating account, opened online
  - ACH and domestic wires
  - Company debit cards
  - Card acceptance (take card payments from customers), planned
- Direction we're exploring: "agentic banking": an AI agent built into the bank that can build custom financial dashboards and automations on top of your account (e.g. set rules like moving money aside for taxes, handle invoices and payouts, categorize spend). Treat this as a forward-looking feature, not something live today.

## Who it's for
- Startup founders and small business owners
- Sole proprietors, freelancers, and solo operators
- Businesses that got rejected or ignored by other business banking apps

## Stage (be honest on the site)
- Pre-launch. Building the product and finalizing banking partners now.
- Pilot planned with about 100 businesses, targeted for Q2 2027.
- No customers, deposits, or usage metrics yet, so the site must not show made-up stats, logos, testimonials, or "trusted by X businesses" claims.

## Legal / compliance copy rules
- Fuime is a financial technology company, not a bank. Banking services will be provided by a partner bank (not yet announced), Member FDIC. Do not name a bank on the site until it's signed, and use a placeholder footer disclosure like:
  "Fuime is a financial technology company, not a bank. Banking services provided by [Partner Bank], Member FDIC." 
- Don't promise FDIC insurance amounts, APYs, fees, or launch dates that aren't confirmed.
- Don't name competitors on the site.
- Legal entity: Ninth Street Labs LLC.
